# Seed505 independent-RL replication: frozen eight-repeat Phase1–2 protocol

Status: registered before six new seed505 gold continuation groups. This is a
second **RL seed**, not eight independent RL seeds. The seed505 U0→U5 policy
update, SkillNet-37 bank, local frozen 0.6B per-state top-1 router, training
batch, seen/unseen evaluations, 404 first-call anchors, and the original two
gold continuation groups are retained. No RL training, old rollout, or external
API call is repeated. The 25 naturally invoked skills remain in scope; counts,
games, and unsupported cells are reported, not used to silently narrow the
population.

The readout registry is the 285-column registry frozen in the completed
seed404 formula expansion. Seed505 computes all of those columns at the fixed
U0 training contexts, using the same U0 and U5 checkpoints and the original
OLD probability archive. Stable FP64 arithmetic and the original BF16/SDPA
model-forward backend are retained. The 261 pre-existing variant columns,
six reference columns, 12 realized-update columns, and 12 factorized reward
columns are committed before any new seed505 utility label is produced. All D
variants and their matched unsigned/reward-free comparators remain in the
registry. No formula, sign, threshold, skill pool, or aggregation is selected
after opening the six new gold repeats.

Utility uses the unchanged first-call anchor per source trajectory. Target
skill, placebo, and null continuations are paired by anchor, endpoint, and
continuation seed. Existing gold seeds 63011 and 63021 are reused; six new
seeds 505, 505100, 505200, 505300, 505400, and 505500 produce eight total
gold groups. The original evidence seed stays separate. The 50-step horizon,
decoding settings, router, 37-skill bank, game weighting, and U0/U5 policies
are unchanged. This gives 21,816 endpoint/arm continuation records: 7,272
reused and 14,544 new. There is no time cap; storage gates and no-retry
failure preservation remain active.

The signed target is decline, `−m = −ΔM`, and the magnitude target is
`|m| = |ΔM|`. The main common candidate pool is the frozen
PLACEBO/stable_raw/all pool, with NULL and stage-stratified analyses retained.
Directional results include average precision, AUROC for decline versus rest
and decline versus increase, Spearman, Kendall, sign accuracy/abstention, and
top-budget metrics, at 0 and 5 percentage point thresholds. Magnitude results
include raw-score and absolute-score transforms, Spearman, Kendall, AP/AUROC
for changes exceeding 5 points, and top-quartile lift. Paired game ×
continuation bootstrap intervals use 2,000 draws; 2/4/8-repeat and
added-six-only curves assess label stability. Every registered score is
reported in machine-readable grids, with a 285-row joined primary table.

Interpretation boundary: seed505 is an independent training update, but its
original two-repeat utility labels were available during exploration of some
formulas. The eight-repeat study checks robustness to additional continuation
noise and another RL seed; it is not a fully blind prospective test, and
bootstrap intervals condition on this seed, candidate pool, and fixed
predictors. The full 285-column search is exploratory and must not be reported
as a single unadjusted confirmatory comparison.
